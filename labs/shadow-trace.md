# Project: Shadow Trace - Incident Response Investigation

**Objective:** Conduct an end-to-end incident investigation to track an adversary's footprint across the network.

### 🔍 Key Skills Demonstrated
* **Evidence Gathering:** Collecting and analyzing artifacts from compromised endpoints.
* **Timeline Analysis:** Reconstructing the attack sequence to identify the point of entry and lateral movement.
* **Malware Analysis:** Analyzing suspicious files found during the investigation to determine intent and impact.

### 🛠 Tools Used
* **SIEM Tools:** Querying logs to identify malicious patterns.
* **Command Line Utilities:** Utilizing advanced CLI commands to parse logs and extract IOCs (Indicators of Compromise).
* **Forensic Frameworks:** Methodical approach to isolating and investigating the intrusion.

### 💡 Core Insight
Shadow Trace reinforced the importance of **Correlation**. An alert in isolation might look like a false positive,
but by correlating log data across different sources, the full scope of the attack becomes clear.
Documentation and methodical evidence tracking are non-negotiable in a professional SOC environment.
