# Project: Introduction to Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)

**Objective:** Understanding how to collect, process, and apply Threat Intelligence to anticipate, identify, and mitigate cyber threats before they impact operations.

### 🔍 Key Skills Demonstrated
* **Intelligence Lifecycles:** Understanding the stages of CTI (Direction, Collection, Processing, Analysis, Dissemination, and Feedback).
* **IOCs vs. TTPs:** Differentiating between standard Indicators of Compromise (IPs, hashes) and adversary behaviors (Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures).
* **Framework Integration:** Leveraging frameworks like **MITRE ATT&CK** to map adversary behavior and build proactive detection rules.

### 🛠 Tools & Concepts Used
* **Open Source Intelligence (OSINT):** Gathering threat data from public feeds and reports.
* **Threat Feeds & STIX/TAXIX:** Understanding how threat intelligence is formatted and shared across the industry.
* **Pyramid of Pain:** Applying the model to understand how disrupting different levels of indicators affects the adversary.

### 💡 Core Insight
This lab highlighted that Threat Intelligence is not just about collecting lists of bad IP addresses—it's about understanding the *adversary's mindset*. Knowing *why* and *how* an attacker operates allows a SOC to shift from a purely reactive stance to proactive threat hunting.
